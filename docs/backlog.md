## Design review: top 10

1. **Payoff within 2 seconds.** Today rests on the frame that shows the problem; Proposed jumps to just before its payoff and holds 2.5 s on it. Most needed on 06, 08 and 19.
2. **Staggered reveal and one spotlight ring.** Today layers leave in 150 ms, Proposed layers enter in causal order, and one ring marks the change on the first tap. Removes overlapping text in 04, 07, 10, 11, 13, 17 and 18.
3. **One-sentence captions** under each scene, with the full text behind "How it works".
4. **One visual grammar.** The world layer stays the same in Today and Proposed (fix 02 and 14), a red ✕ verdict becomes a green ✓ in the same place, and a dashed ghost of Today stays in Proposed.
5. **A fast lane** into the three ideas to start with, and into suggested ideas without placing stars.
6. **Scene first on phones.** Title, known line, scene, moment, ask, then the rest.
7. **Legible at arm's length.** Scene text at 13 px and above, at most two lines in a phone panel.
8. **Signature animations** for the three "Short on time" ideas: 06 ask points and line of sight, 07 alert reach in both directions, 13 a deadline gate on the route.
9. **Three beats per idea** in the walkthrough (known and today, try, ask), with the scene pinned and the rest behind "More about this idea".
10. **The picture is the control.** Lanes (09), arrows on the road (11), answer chips (10), Yes and No (06) and the gate (13) become tap targets in the scene.
